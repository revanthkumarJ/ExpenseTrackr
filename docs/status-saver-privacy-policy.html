Status Saver — No Ads — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026
In short: Status Saver collects nothing. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, and no crash reporting. The app does not even hold the Android internet permission, so it is technically incapable of sending your files or any information anywhere.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Status Saver — No Ads Android application ("Status Saver", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

None. Status Saver has no user accounts, no sign-up, and shows no advertising. We do not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, device identifiers, usage analytics, or advertising IDs. We never access, upload, or share the statuses you view or save.

2. No internet access

Status Saver does not declare the Android INTERNET permission. Android itself therefore prevents the app from opening any network connection. There is no server, no cloud storage, no analytics service and no crash-reporting service behind this app — not because we promise not to use one, but because the app has no way to reach one. You can confirm this in the app's permission list on the Play Store or in your device settings.

3. Permissions we request and why

  • Access to the WhatsApp statuses folder: on Android 10 and newer, the app asks you once to select WhatsApp's .Statuses folder using Android's own folder picker. The app reads that folder to list the statuses you have already viewed. The permission stays on your device and can be revoked at any time in Android settings.
  • Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, on Android 9 and older only): to read the statuses folder and to copy the files you choose into your Downloads folder.
  • App visibility (queries): so the app can tell whether WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business is installed, open it when you tap "Open WhatsApp", and send a status back to it when you tap "Repost".
  • Wake lock (WAKE_LOCK): used by the bundled video player component so the screen behaves correctly while a video status is playing.
  • Network state (ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): declared by a bundled media library. Because the app has no internet permission, no network activity is possible and this permission has no effect on your data.

These permissions are used solely to make the app work and for no other purpose.

4. Where your files are stored

Statuses you save are copied into the public Downloads folder on your own device, under Download/StatusSaver/WhatsApp. They belong to you: they are visible in your gallery and file manager, and they remain on your device even if you uninstall Status Saver. The only other thing the app stores is a small local preferences file holding your theme choice and the folder you selected. Uninstalling the app deletes that file.

5. Sharing and selling data

We do not sell, share, or transfer any data, because we do not have any. When you tap "Share" or "Repost", Android hands the selected file to the app you pick (for example WhatsApp); from that point the receiving app's own privacy policy applies. When you tap "More apps", "Rate on Play Store", or a privacy link, Android opens the Google Play Store or your browser, which are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

6. Data retention

We hold no data, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Files you save and the app's local preferences stay on your device until you delete them, delete the saved files, or uninstall the app.

7. Children's privacy

Status Saver is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

8. Security

The app keeps everything on your device and transmits nothing, so there is no account to breach and no server holding your information.

9. Content ownership

Status Saver does not download, host, or distribute any content itself. It only gives you access to media that WhatsApp has already stored on your device after you viewed it. Statuses belong to the people who posted them. Please respect their rights and obtain permission before re-sharing or re-posting someone else's content.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be reflected here with a new "Last updated" date, and, where appropriate, noted in the app.
