Puzzle Box — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026
In short: Puzzle Box is a collection of offline puzzles, arcade games and games to play with friends on one phone. It has no accounts, no in-app purchases and no chat. Your progress, coins and scores are stored only on your phone. The app sends anonymous diagnostics — crash reports and gameplay counts such as which game was opened and how long a level took — through Google Firebase, so bugs can be fixed and the games improved. It does not use your advertising ID.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Puzzle Box Android application ("Puzzle Box", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

1. What we do not collect

There are no user accounts and no sign-up. We do not collect, store or transmit your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, location or payment details. The app has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions, and no chat or social features — words typed in Word Guess are used only on your phone for that round.

2. What the app does send

Puzzle Box uses two Google Firebase services: Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase. Both are anonymous and are used only to keep the app working well.

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

If the app crashes, Crashlytics sends a report containing the technical stack trace, your device model, Android version, the app version, the time, the game and level that were open, and a list of the last few game actions before the crash, along with a randomly generated installation identifier that is not linked to you and is reset if you reinstall the app.

Google Analytics for Firebase (usage counts)

Analytics counts how the games are played so we can see which ones people enjoy and where they get stuck. It records events such as:

  • which game was opened and how long you stayed on it;
  • starting, restarting and finishing a puzzle level, with the level number, moves, time taken and hints used;
  • starting and finishing a play-together game, with the number of players, how many seats were the computer and whether a person or the computer won;
  • arcade scores and whether a new best score was set;
  • searching the game list (the search words you typed), claiming the daily gift and finishing the daily challenge;
  • the local hour of day and day of the week of each event, so we can see when games are played.

Analytics also collects standard technical information: app version, device model, Android version, language, country (estimated from the network) and a random app-instance identifier. The advertising ID is not collected and ad-personalisation signals are turned off.

3. Data stored on your device only

Your level progress, coins, daily-challenge streak, best scores, achievements and settings are saved in a small private file on your phone. They never leave your device, are not linked to any account and are deleted when you uninstall the app (or may be restored by Android's own device backup, if you have it turned on).

4. Permissions

  • Internet (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used only to deliver the anonymous diagnostics above. Every game works fully offline; diagnostics are queued and sent later when a connection is available.
  • Wake lock (WAKE_LOCK): added by the Firebase library so diagnostics can be delivered reliably in the background.
  • Install referrer (BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE): added by Firebase so Google Play can tell Analytics that an install happened. It reads nothing from your device.

5. Sharing and selling data

We do not sell or trade any data. The anonymous diagnostics are processed on our behalf by Google as part of the Firebase platform under Firebase's privacy terms and the Google Privacy Policy, and are visible only to the app's developer as aggregate counts and crash reports.

6. Data retention

We run no server of our own. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days, and user-level Analytics data expires on a rolling window (two months by default), after which only aggregated, non-identifying counts remain. Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app.

7. Your choices

Because the diagnostics are anonymous and carry no account, there is nothing tied to you for us to look up, export or delete. Uninstalling Puzzle Box stops all collection immediately, and you can block the app's network access in Android settings without losing any feature. For any question or request, email the address in section 11.

8. Children's privacy

Puzzle Box is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Security

There are no accounts to breach and no server of ours holding your data. Diagnostics are sent to Google over an encrypted connection.
