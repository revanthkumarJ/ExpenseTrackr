Puzzle Box — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026
In short: Puzzle Box is a collection of offline puzzles, arcade games and games to play with friends on one phone. It has no accounts, no in-app purchases and no chat. Your progress, coins and scores are stored only on your phone. The app sends anonymous diagnostics — crash reports and gameplay counts such as which game was opened and how long a level took — through Google Firebase, so bugs can be fixed and the games improved. The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, which uses your device's advertising ID; in the EEA, UK and Switzerland it asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Puzzle Box Android application ("Puzzle Box", "the app", "we", "us") handles your information. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

1. What we do not collect

There are no user accounts and no sign-up. We do not collect, store or transmit your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, location or payment details. The app has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions, and no chat or social features — words typed in Word Guess are used only on your phone for that round.

2. What the app does send

Puzzle Box uses two Google Firebase services: Firebase Crashlytics and Google Analytics for Firebase. Both are anonymous and are used only to keep the app working well.

Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

If the app crashes, Crashlytics sends a report containing the technical stack trace, your device model, Android version, the app version, the time, the game and level that were open, and a list of the last few game actions before the crash, along with a randomly generated installation identifier that is not linked to you and is reset if you reinstall the app.

Google Analytics for Firebase (usage counts)

Analytics counts how the games are played so we can see which ones people enjoy and where they get stuck. It records events such as:

  • which game was opened and how long you stayed on it;
  • starting, restarting and finishing a puzzle level, with the level number, moves, time taken and hints used;
  • starting and finishing a play-together game, with the number of players, how many seats were the computer and whether a person or the computer won;
  • arcade scores and whether a new best score was set;
  • searching the game list (the search words you typed), claiming the daily gift and finishing the daily challenge;
  • the local hour of day and day of the week of each event, so we can see when games are played.

Analytics also collects standard technical information: app version, device model, Android version, language, country (estimated from the network) and a random app-instance identifier. Analytics' own advertising-ID collection and ad-personalisation signals are turned off, so Analytics never links your play to an ad profile (AdMob's use of the advertising ID for ads is described below).

Google AdMob (advertising)

Puzzle Box is free and is paid for by ads served by Google AdMob. Ads appear only in a few places, never on a game board or while you are playing:

  • a banner below the card shown when a game ends (level solved, winner, game over);
  • an ad tile in the list of games on the home screen, marked "Ad";
  • a rewarded video, only if you choose it, when you run out of coins for a hint — watching it adds coins.

To choose, show and measure ads and to protect against ad fraud, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location (from the IP address), device and app information, and how you interact with ads. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. No game progress, typed words or other content from the app is shared with advertisers.

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. In US states with privacy laws, you can opt out of the sale or sharing of your data through Google's US privacy message. You can review or change your choice any time with the Ad privacy button on the Trophies & stats screen (shown where it applies).

Analytics records when a rewarded ad was watched and how many coins it gave, without any advertising data. Google Analytics for Firebase itself still does not collect the advertising ID; only AdMob uses it, for ads.

3. Data stored on your device only

Your level progress, coins, daily-challenge streak, best scores, achievements and settings are saved in a small private file on your phone. They never leave your device, are not linked to any account and are deleted when you uninstall the app (or may be restored by Android's own device backup, if you have it turned on).

4. Permissions

  • Internet (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): used only to deliver the anonymous diagnostics above and to load ads. Every game works fully offline; diagnostics are queued and sent later when a connection is available, and no ads load while offline.
  • Wake lock (WAKE_LOCK): added by the Firebase library so diagnostics can be delivered reliably in the background.
  • Install referrer (BIND_GET_INSTALL_REFERRER_SERVICE): added by Firebase so Google Play can tell Analytics that an install happened. It reads nothing from your device.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) and Android's privacy-preserving ads permissions (ACCESS_ADSERVICES_AD_ID, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_ATTRIBUTION, ACCESS_ADSERVICES_TOPICS): added by the Google Mobile Ads SDK so AdMob can show and measure ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE): added by Google libraries for short background tasks; the app does not run anything in the background on its own.

5. Sharing and selling data

We do not sell or trade any data. The anonymous diagnostics are processed on our behalf by Google as part of the Firebase platform under Firebase's privacy terms and the Google Privacy Policy, and are visible only to the app's developer as aggregate counts and crash reports. Advertising data is collected and processed by Google AdMob and its advertising partners as described in section 2.

6. Data retention

We run no server of our own. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days, and user-level Analytics data expires on a rolling window (two months by default), after which only aggregated, non-identifying counts remain. Advertising data is kept by Google under its own retention policies. Data on your device stays until you uninstall the app.

7. Your choices

Because the diagnostics are anonymous and carry no account, there is nothing tied to you for us to look up, export or delete. For ads, you can change your consent with the Ad privacy button (where offered), and reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Uninstalling Puzzle Box stops all collection immediately, and you can block the app's network access in Android settings without losing any game. For any question or request, email the address in section 11.

8. Children's privacy

Puzzle Box is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Security

There are no accounts to breach and no server of ours holding your data. Diagnostics are sent to Google over an encrypted connection.
